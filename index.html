A small shop, fully stocked

A corner of George Street stacked with the staples that make jollof, egusi, brown stew chicken, ackee & saltfish, and pounded yam taste the way they should. Fresh produce, store-cupboard essentials, frozen fish and meats, drinks, and the spices that travel a long way to get here — and don't sit on the shelf for long.
